## Proposed Spec Changes

Changes that need approval *before* proceeding: weakening, redefining or substituting an
acceptance criterion, or changing the failure policy. Propose here; do not silently rewrite
the dossier.

## Scope Amendments

Widenings of the declared `write-scope` block, which may proceed once declared and logged.
One entry per amendment: the date, the paths added, and the criterion that required them.
Distinct from `## Proposed Spec Changes` above because an amendment is a completed action
being recorded, not a request awaiting a decision.
